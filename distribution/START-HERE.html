Welcome to ASIN Pulse

One-time Chrome setup. After this, use the two desktop shortcuts.

  1. In the Chrome Extensions tab that just opened, turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  2. Click Load unpacked.
  3. Choose this folder. You can paste the path into the folder picker:__INSTALL_FOLDER__
  4. Open ASIN Pulse from your desktop.
Future updates take one double-click.

Finish or pause a scan, then double-click Update ASIN Pulse on your desktop. It downloads the latest release and asks the extension to reload itself. Your scans and settings stay in Chrome.

If the extension is not installed in the Chrome profile that opens, repeat these setup steps in that profile. Chrome may remind you that this extension is loaded in Developer mode.
