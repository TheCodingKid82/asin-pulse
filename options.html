ACTIVE · FREE

Amazon purchase signal

No account or API key needed. Open a product on Amazon.com, Amazon.ca, or Amazon.co.uk with the page language set to English. Click the green ASIN Pulse button at the bottom right or the Chrome toolbar icon.

“1K+ bought in past month” is a broad, rounded estimate of customers who bought the product. It is not an exact count of units, a calendar-month report, or a forecast of your sales. Some products have no badge.

OPTIONAL

Keepa data access

If you add a Keepa API subscription later, you can retrieve its saved Amazon purchase signal for the selected ASIN. Keepa does not make this an exact unit count and often has no monthly signal either.

Lookups happen only when you click Check Keepa data. Each uncached lookup uses your Keepa API tokens. Results are cached locally for 24 hours (1 hour if unavailable). The extension requests existing data without forcing an Amazon refresh.

Stored locally in this Chrome profile, not synced. Sent only to api.keepa.com when you request a lookup. Amazon pages cannot read the saved key.

What stays on your computer

The free version reads the currently displayed product page. It sends no analytics, product lists, or browsing history to a server. CSV exports are saved by your browser. Optional Keepa requests send your key, selected ASIN, and marketplace directly to Keepa.
